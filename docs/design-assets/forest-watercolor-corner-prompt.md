# Forest watercolor corner

Tool: built-in imagegen. Transparent PNG. Style reference: existing `tree-root-meadow.png`.

Final project asset: `apps/web/public/assets/forest/forest-watercolor-corner.png`.

## Final prompt

Generate a single production-ready transparent botanical corner vignette for NoteBuddy, a scholarly watercolor knowledge forest canvas. Reference image provides botanical watercolor style and palette ONLY; do not reproduce the whole scene or UI. A few delicate wild olive/sage leaves on fine naturally bending stems grow from the LOWER LEFT corner and drift a little along the left and bottom edges. Tiny muted ochre seed dots and pale watercolor pigment blooms, softly feathered edges. Subdued sage gray-green, dusty olive, warm parchment undertones. The decoration should feel airy and elegant, like a faint botanical illustration at a book margin, visibly hand painted but modest in density. Keep upper right and most of the center completely transparent. No landscape, campus, horizon, grass floor, tree trunk, complete tree, stone plaque, text, symbols, box, frame, solid background or hard border. Fully isolated transparent PNG, portrait-ish canvas about 4:5 with the plant weighted in the lower left. Enough visible leaf texture for a 250px display, while all surroundings dissolve to transparency. This will be rendered as a quiet fixed corner wash behind independently zoomable real trees; it must not imply a fixed ground line. The provided image is a STYLE AND PIGMENT reference only. Create leafy stems in a CORNER, not a ground bed. No soil strip, no grass mat and no landscape. The leaves should be slender and delicate, watercolor silhouettes suitable for a very pale margin decoration.
